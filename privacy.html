Privacy Policy

Updated November 15, 2026

This website presents the apps made by Visto Apps. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no ads, and it does not ask for personal data. Fonts and images are served from the same server, so your visit is not shared with other companies.

The only preference saved is the language you pick (English or Romanian), in your own browser, and you can clear it from your browser settings at any time.

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply to you. Each app has its own privacy policy, linked from its App Store page.

For questions about your data, write to the address on the contact page.
